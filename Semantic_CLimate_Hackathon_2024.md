
The 2-day hackathon organized by Semantic Climate united innovators, programmers, scientists, and climate activists to tackle the urgent issue of climate change. The event emphasized the need for immediate, innovative solutions to combat the environmental crisis. Over two days, participants used Python toolkits to extract data from verbose IPCC reports for meaningful applications. On the first day, interdisciplinary teams formed, combining diverse skills to brainstorm and conceptualize projects to mitigate climate change. On the second day, teams developed their ideas into functional prototypes, with mentors providing guidance to ensure feasibility and impact. The urgency of addressing climate issues was a recurring theme, and participants showcased solutions driving immediate action. The hackathon concluded with an awards ceremony recognizing the most promising projects.
